ICT Server Administration

Overview of Computer Software Issues

ICT server administration is the practice of setting up, managing, securing, and maintaining computer servers—both physical machines and virtual software—so that an organization's data, network services, and applications run safely, smoothly, and without unexpected downtime

Software issues are bugs, errors, or system flaws that cause unexpected behavior, crashes, or security vulnerabilities. They stem from coding mistakes, poor design, bad server setups, or unclear project requirements. Fixing them requires testing, debugging tools, and regular system updates

Common Types of Software Issues

  • Bugs and Logic Errors: Code fails to run right or gives wrong answers because of a mistake in the program rules.
  • Performance Bottlenecks: Slow loading times or frozen screens happen due to heavy database loads or bad code.
  • Security Vulnerabilities: Weak passwords, unpatched systems, or exposed data create openings for cyberattacks.
  • Compatibility Conflicts: Programs fail to work properly with older operating systems or different hardware

📌 Occupational Therapy Services

Occupational therapy (OT) is treatment that helps you improve your ability to perform daily tasks. It can help you learn how to move through your environment or use different tools to participate in your activities safely.

You might need OT while you’re recovering from an injury or trauma, including:

  • Bone fractures.
  • Burns.
  • Carpal tunnel syndrome.
  • Strokes.
  • Amputation.
  • Traumatic brain injuries (TBIs).
  • Spinal cord injuries.
  • Hand injuries.

📌 Physiotherapy Medical Services

Physiotherapy is a clinical health science that assesses, diagnoses, and treats physical conditions, injuries, and movement dysfunctions. It uses physical methods like exercise, movement, and manual therapy to restore function, reduce pain, and improve overall health and mobility

Core Medical Areas Treated including the following;

  • Musculoskeletal: Back pain, neck pain, arthritis, sprains, and sports injuries.
  • Neurological: Stroke recovery, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis.
  • Cardiorespiratory: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and heart attack rehabilitation.
  • Pelvic & Specialized: Bladder incontinence, pre/postnatal care, and post-surgical recovery.

📌 Details on Physiotherapy Medical Services

Physical therapy (PT), also known as physiotherapy, is a healthcare profession, as well as the care provided by physical therapists. It focuses on promoting, maintaining, or restoring health through patient education, physical interventions, disease prevention, and health promotion. The term physical therapist or physiotherapist is used to represent the trained person providing physical therapy. The profession has many specialties including musculoskeletal, orthopedics, cardiopulmonary, neurology, endocrinology, sports medicine, geriatrics, pediatrics, women's health, wound care and electromyography. PTs practice in many settings, both public and private.[1]

Occupational Therapy focuses on helping people overcome challenges from illnesses or injury. For this major you’ll take lots of occupational therapy classes.
