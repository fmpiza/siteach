📌 Ante Natal Services

Ante Natal Services health check-ups for pregnant women. The World Health Organization recommends at least eight clinic visits during pregnancy to track the health of the mother and the baby, prevent illness, and prepare for a safe birth. 📌 Laborward Services

Labor Ward Services are specialized, 24/7 hospital departments dedicated to supporting women through the critical stages of childbirth, from early labor to immediate postpartum recovery. They are designed to manage both standard, low-risk deliveries and complex obstetric emergencies. The following is a list of Laborward Services obtained in Arusha City Hospital

📌 Post Natal Services

📌 Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT) Services

Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT) Services diagnose and treat conditions of the head and neck, including hearing loss, sinus infections, tonsillitis, and sleep apnea. Specialists use medical and surgical care to help patients breathe, hear, and swallow well. The following is a list of ENT Services obtained in Arusha City Hospital

View

📌 Orthopaedic Medical Services

Orthopaedic medical services focus on the diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of the musculoskeletal system, covering bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, muscles, and nerves. Care ranges from non-surgical physical therapy and joint injections to major surgeries like total joint replacements and trauma repair. The following is a list of Orthopaedic Medical Services obtained in Arusha City Hospital

View

📌 Surgery Medical Services

Medical surgery services involve operations and procedures that cut, fix, or explore body tissues to diagnose, treat, or prevent health issues. These services range from minor outpatient procedures to major inpatient operations requiring a skilled team of medical professionals and specialized recovery care. The following is a list of Surgery Medical Services obtained in Arusha City Hospital

View

📌 Out Patient (OPD) Services

An Outpatient Department (OPD) is the part of a hospital or clinic where you get medical care, tests, and doctor visits without staying overnight. It serves as your main first step for non-emergency health needs, routine checks, and follow-up care. The following is a list of Out Patients (OPD) Services obtained in Arusha City Hospital

View

📌 In Patient (IPD) Services

In Patient (IPD) Services provides hospital care for patients who need to stay overnight or longer for 24 hours or more. It involves continuous medical supervision, nursing support, and treatment for severe illnesses, childbirth, or major surgeries that require round-the-clock monitoring. 📌 Radiology Investigations

Radiology investigation uses imaging technology like X-rays, CT scans, and MRIs to look inside the body, find health issues, and guide treatments. Doctors use these safe tests to spot broken bones, check organ health, and find diseases early. The following is a list of Radiology Investigations obtained in Arusha City Hospital

View

📌 Eye Medical Services

Eye Medical Services professional checks, medical treatments, and surgeries to care for your eyes and vision. These services are provided by trained The Eye Care Team members like Ophthalmologists and Optometrists to treat issues ranging from simple blurry vision to complex diseases. The following is a list of Eye Medical Services obtained in Arusha City Hospital

📌 Dental Medical Services

Dental Medical Services cover the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of conditions related to the teeth, gums, and mouth. Core care ranges from routine cleanings and check-ups to cavity fillings, root canals, tooth extractions, and major restorative options like crowns, bridges, and dentures. Minor theatre services refer to specialized outpatient medical areas designed for less complex surgical procedures.
